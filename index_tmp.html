Background

My undergraduate degree is in mathematics from Franklin College and my PhD is from the Department of Statistics at Purdue University, co-advised by Jayanta K. Ghosh and Chuanhai Liu. Before joining the Department of Statistics at NC State, I spent five years in the Department of Mathematics, Statistics, and Computer Science at University of Illinois–Chicago.

Researchers.One

Harry Crane and I are co-founders of an online platform for peer review and scholarly publication. For details, please visit www.researchers.one and follow us on Twitter, @ResearchersOne.

Research interests

This list varies over time but, for now, my research interests include: foundations of statistics, generalized Bayes and Gibbs posterior distributions, high-dimensional problems, imprecise probability, mixture models, etc.
